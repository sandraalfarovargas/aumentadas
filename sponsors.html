Frequently asked questions

What is Aumentadas?

A creative lab where experienced women professionals recognise the value of what they do well in their craft and use AI to turn it into solutions, products and opportunities.

We're not an AI academy. The starting point is what each participant already does well in her craft.

How is Aumentadas different from other AI programmes?

Most teach tools or train technical profiles. At Aumentadas each participant starts with her craft and creates a solution of her own.

And we measure what gets created without inflating results.

Who takes part?

Spanish-speaking women professionals living in Spain. They're employed or self-employed, job hunting or in a career transition.

Some work below their qualifications. Others see AI transforming their role. Others want to create something of their own and don't know where to start.

How are they selected?

They apply through a form and, if needed, talk with Sandra. We look at their experience, motivation and commitment. They don't need to code or have a defined idea.

What concrete results do we get?

Each participant works towards leaving the Lab with a basic app that solves a real problem, plus her PIA Profile. In January you receive your scholar's project and the Lab's impact report.

The first cohort's goals are hypotheses to measure, not guaranteed results.

What visibility does our organisation get?
  • Logo on aumentadas.com and on the Lab page.
  • Mention and tag on Instagram and LinkedIn.
  • Photos and video of the Lab for your channels.
  • Impact report: what participants created with AI.

Companies that fund scholarships for women on their team also appear as a sponsoring company.

Can we see the projects?

Yes, with their creators' permission. The projects belong to the participants and we protect their intellectual property.

Can we contribute without money?

Yes. For the Lab on 5 December we're looking for:

  • A venue: a room for 20 people with Wi-Fi, projector, tables and power outlets, 9:00 to 18:00.
  • Mentors: professionals in AI, product, business or marketing, on the day of the Lab or in the follow-up.
  • Coffee break and lunch: for 20 people.
How is sponsoring different from hiring a programme?

If you sponsor, you fund places or resources for the open Lab and receive visibility and an impact report. If you hire, we run an edition tailored to your community. See options for organisations.

What if a participant doesn't manage to create a solution?

Aumentadas doesn't guarantee a product, clients, income or employment. The first cohort is for measuring what works.

A participant may finish with a prototype, a validated solution, a new professional offer or the lesson that an idea doesn't have enough value. Finding that out early is also validation.

Is it a tax-deductible donation?

No. Aumentadas isn't a non-profit and doesn't issue donation certificates. You pay through the form and Sandra issues an invoice.

We're in the process of setting up the organisation that will run this project.

Why support it now rather than once it's more established?

Because the first cohort is when the model is built and validated. The first organisations bring credibility and help shape how Aumentadas grows.

Can we talk before deciding?

Yes. Does a 15-minute call work for you? Fill in the sponsorship form or message me on WhatsApp.
